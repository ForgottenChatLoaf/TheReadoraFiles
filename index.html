Our Story

About Readora

Readora was born from a simple belief: every person deserves access to extraordinary books without limits. Founded in 2021, we set out to build more than a bookstore — a community of passionate readers united by the transformative power of storytelling. From dog-eared paperbacks to pristine hardcovers, each title is sourced with care.

Today, we serve over 50,000 readers across the Philippines and beyond, with a curated catalogue of more than 1,200 titles spanning every genre imaginable. Our team of in-house bibliophiles personally reviews each book — ensuring whatever lands on your shelf is worthy of your time. We're not just selling books, we're opening doors to new worlds.
