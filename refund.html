Customer Protection

Refund & Returns Policy

We want you to love every purchase. Here's everything you need to know about our returns process.

Demo Project Notice: Readora™ is a student project created for educational purposes only. No real transactions are processed, no actual orders are placed, and no real payments are collected. This refund policy is presented for demonstration purposes only.

Eligibility for Returns

We accept returns on most items within 7 days of the delivery date, provided the following conditions are met:

  • Item is in its original, unused condition
  • Item is in its original packaging with all tags intact
  • Return is requested within 7 days of receiving the order
  • Proof of purchase (order number) is provided
  • Item is not listed under Non-Returnable Items below

Non-Returnable Items

The following items are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged under any circumstances:

Digital Products E-Books Gift Cards Sale / Clearance Items Opened Stationery Personalized Items Downloadable Content Subscription Plans

How to Request a Refund

Follow these steps to submit a return or refund request:

1
Contact Support Fill out the Contact Us form below or email us at support@readora.com with your order number and reason for return.
2
Receive Confirmation Our team will review your request and send a confirmation email within 1–2 business days with return instructions.
3
Ship the Item Back Pack the item securely and ship it to our returns address provided in the confirmation email. Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item is damaged or incorrect.
4
Refund Processed Once we receive and inspect the returned item, your refund will be issued to your original payment method.

Refund Timeline

Once your returned item has been received and inspected by our team, refunds are typically processed within:

5–7 Business Days

Please note that your bank or payment provider may take additional time to reflect the refund in your account. GCash and Maya refunds may appear within 3–5 business days. PayPal refunds are typically instant.

Damaged or Wrong Items

If you received a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, we sincerely apologize. Please follow these steps:

  • Take clear photos of the damaged or incorrect item immediately upon receipt
  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your photos and order number
  • We will arrange a free return pickup or provide a prepaid shipping label
  • A full refund or replacement will be issued at no extra cost to you

Contact Us

Have a question about your return? Fill out the form below and our support team will get back to you within 1–2 business days.
